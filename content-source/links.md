## Lithuania — Dances

| # | Video | Title / Theme | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PNtlBWSjBnE | Linksmasis šokis | Social dance, not staged for an audience — a "dance for the dancers." Helped bond rural communities; for young people specifically, a way to meet, court, and find a partner. |
| 2 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bJxlcGJEG6w | Round dance (children's) | Classified as a circle game / children's-youth folk dance. The youngest performers in Lithuanian folk choreography are called "spirgučiai" (also the affectionate name for the youngest dancer groups at Lithuania's Song Festival) — the dance itself is lively, energetic, playful. |
| 3 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqQItZLiFS8 | Round dance (children's) — variant | Same "spirgučiai" children's circle-dance category; energetic, playful dynamic imitating children's lively movement. *(Source reused this same video link across 3 different entries — likely a copy/paste artifact from the original email; same video assigned to all 3 per instruction.)* |
| 4 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BqQItZLiFS8 | Blezdingėlė | Old Lithuanian folk dance originating in Žemaitija, imitating the flight of swallows. Name comes from the Žemaitian word for swallow, "blezdinga." |
| 5 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4neeFA--Tps | (Ländler-type dance) | Adopted from a neighboring country (likely Germany or Prussia) — a "Ländler"-type dance, over time fully "Lithuanianized" into a distinctly Žemaitian character. |
| 6 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CYc_albY-2k | Dobilėlis ("Kad aug āboliņš" / "As the clover grows") | Žemaitian agrarian/work dance-game reflecting the farmer's bond with nature and fieldwork. Clover cultivation was vital for feeding livestock, so a good clover harvest symbolized a farm's prosperity and luck. |

## Lithuania — Songs, Batch 2 ("Lietuvas dziesmas")

| # | Video | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8eE2MxX7hoA | Išjos brolutėlis, sodauto | A *sutartinė* (polyphonic Lithuanian folk-song style) from the Aukštaitija region — a dialogue between brother and sister. The sister asks what he'll bring her from Riga; he promises a gold ring. |
| 2 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cyA6Ekh70hA | Oi Šermukšnio | About a young soldier going to serve in the army, his journey, and his fight against the enemy — reflects soldiers' courage and resolve. |
| 3 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jxk0aRv-gwo | Mona bolundele | Old Žemaitian folk song asking a neighbor not to scare away the pigeons. The pigeons — peaceful birds living near people's homes, feeding on grain, laying eggs, raising young — symbolize family harmony, life, and its continuation. Gentle, playful interplay between people and nature. |
| 4 | https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlBKWpXPjjo | Ne bet kokia aš mergelė buvau | From the Dzūkija region — a young woman proud of her selectiveness, choosing handsome, well-dressed young men. Dancing with a young man, she feels joyful, as if the ground trembles and a rue flower blooms on her head, her rings gleaming. Dancing with a widower, her fate is reversed — the ground seems to collapse, the rue wilts, the rings rust. |
